title
The Mention Typing Ontology (MiTO)
description
The **_Mention Typing Ontology (MiTO)_** is an OWL 2 DL ontology designed to formalize the concept of mentions, which are crucial for bibliometric analysis, as they allow researchers to track references to entities (e.g., software) in scholarly communication. This ontology aims to enhance interoperability with other SPAR ontologies by providing a flexible framework for describing mentions and their characteristics. Mentions can be explicit (e.g., directly stated references in text, as when stated into an article _Dr. Johnson's groundbreaking research on climate change_) or implicit (e.g., indirect references to entities or concepts, such as discussing "seminal studies in the field"). MiTO introduces the following key concepts: * **Mention**, which represents the act of referring to or introducing the name of a person, entity, or concept in discourse. This class is defined with minimal restrictions to maximize usability across different contexts. * **MentionType**, which reifies the implicit or explicit nature of a mention, enabling users to classify mentions based on their characteristics. MiTO also defines several object properties to describe relationships between mentions and entities: * `mito:mentions` and its inverse `mito:isMentionedBy`, which connect the two sides involved in the act of mentioning. * `mito:hasMentionedEntity`, which relates a mention to the entity being mentioned. * `mito:hasMentioningEntity`, which relates a mention to the entity performing the act of mentioning. * `mito:hasMentionType`, which defines the implicit or explicit character of the mention. ![The architecture of MiTO.](mito.png) MiTO is designed to integrate with other SPAR ontologies, which provides a modular framework for describing crucial aspects of bibliographic entities and their metadata. ## Examples of use In the following subsections, we introduce some examples to showcase how to use MiTO.
